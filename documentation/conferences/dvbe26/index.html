A governance-aware execution path

1 — DiscoverSystems, owners, dependencies
2 — ClassifyImpact and authority
3 — ProposeChange and alternatives
4 — ProveTests and impact evidence
5 — DecideApprove, modify, escalate
6 — ExecuteWithin authorized boundaries
7 — ObserveCompare outcome to prediction
8 — AuditPreserve decision and result

EU AI Act: oversight is an engineering concern

Article 14 requires effective human oversight for high-risk AI systems.

The human must be enabled to

Understand limitations
Recognize anomalies
Avoid automation bias

The system must support

Interpretation
Override or reversal
Safe interruption

These are architectural capabilities, not an approval-screen feature.

Four moves

1 — Specify

Do not accelerate ambiguity.

2 — Discover

The repository is not always the system boundary.

3 — Control

Match human authority to consequence.

4 — Validate

Prove the impact, not only the implementation.

Takeaways

  1. GenAI accelerates implementation, not organizational understanding.
  2. Specify intent and engineer the workflow before generating.
  3. Discover dependencies, owners, and risks beyond the repository.
  4. Scale human control with impact, autonomy, uncertainty, and irreversibility.
  5. Connect agent actions, human decisions, evidence, and outcomes in one audit trail.
